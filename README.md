# Dupedia website

A static, multi-page site generated by one Python script (standard library only, no npm, no framework), following the same convention as the earlier Dupedia site. Strategy, sitemap and keyword plan: [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md).

## Run it

```bash
python3 src/build.py                 # writes public/ and prints any missing business facts
python3 qa/check_site.py             # static checks: links, titles, descriptions, h1, alt, labels, JSON-LD, sitemap
cd public && python3 -m http.server 8000   # open http://localhost:8000
```

`public/` is the deployable folder. Upload it as-is to Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or any static host. `_headers` sets cache and security headers on Cloudflare Pages and Netlify. `404.html` is picked up automatically by both.

## What's in it

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/` | **Intro loader** (first visit per session), a hero of **draggable brand blocks** that snap to a grid, a lime marquee, a statement that lights up word by word, a product visual that expands as you scroll, the five divisions in a **pinned horizontal scroll**, **"Will it fit?"** (unchanged), a drag-to-scroll product rail, the 7.8× safety-zone insight on a lime band, the process, sectors with a cursor-following image, and the quote CTA |
| `/playgrounds/` | All 59 products, server-rendered and crawlable. Filters by collection, age, site size ("only what fits"), search and sort. Filters are kept in the URL so a view can be shared |
| `/playgrounds/<code-name>/` ×59 | Spec page: dimensions, platform height, safety zone, age, contents, finishes, a fit check drawn to scale, shortlist, related products, Product schema |
| `/what-we-build/`, `/who-we-work-with/`, `/planning-guide/`, `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/privacy/` | See STRATEGY.md §4 |

**Shortlist:** "+" on any product adds it to a shortlist kept in the visitor's browser. It shows in the header and goes with the quote request.

**Look:** charcoal ground (#1B1B19) with cream type, lime accents and one lime band. The logo uses a **reversed version** (cream wordmark, lime mark), made with `tools/reverse_logo.py` from the master PNGs. This was the owner's decision on 7 Nov 2026 and departs from the brand book's "logo never changes" rule. The masters in `static/brand/` are untouched.

**Motion:** `src/assets/motion.js` holds the intro loader (real asset progress, pointer-reactive blocks, click to bounce, Skip/Esc/Enter), the block playground (drag, arrow keys, Enter to turn, Rearrange), the scroll-speed marquee, word-by-word statement, expanding showcase, pinned horizontal divisions, drag rail with inertia, sector image preview, cursor ring with labels, and magnetic buttons. Pages cross-fade (View Transitions). With `prefers-reduced-motion` the loader is skipped, scroll effects show their end state, and the custom cursor is off. Content is visible without JavaScript, and a timer in the page head clears the loader even if scripts fail.

## Configure (src/config.json)

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| `siteUrl` | **Not set yet.** While it is `null` the build is a *review build*: every page is `noindex`, `robots.txt` disallows all, and canonicals use the placeholder domain. Set the real domain (e.g. `https://www.example.com`) and rebuild to publish. |
| `contact.*`, `social.*` | `null` renders a visible `[… · TBC]` placeholder. Fill in and rebuild. |
| `formEndpoint` | **Not set.** Until it is, the form validates, then shows the visitor their request as text to copy and says clearly that it was **not sent**. Set a URL that accepts a JSON `POST` (Formspree, Basin, a Cloudflare Worker, your CRM's webhook) and rebuild. Fields sent: name, company, email, phone, role, projectType, location, site, timing, message, shortlist, reference, page, sentAt. |
| `analytics.plausibleDomain` | Off. No analytics or tracking cookies run, so no consent banner is needed. The privacy page text follows this setting. A script tag still needs adding to `page()` in `build.py` if you turn it on. |
| `clientsCredit` | `"dupedia"`: the 15 client names (from soliwood.org, 7 Nov 2026) show as Dupedia clients in two moving rows on the home and About pages. You confirmed on 7 Nov 2026 that Dupedia delivered these projects. Set it to `"manufacturer"` to credit Soliwood instead. Client names live in `src/data/products.json` → `clients`. |
| `serviceArea` | `["Egypt"]`, used in structured data. Add countries only once they are confirmed. |

## Missing facts and decisions (needed before going public)

1. **Domain** (`siteUrl`). Without it the site stays noindex.
2. **Contact details:** sales phone, WhatsApp, email, office address and city, reply time, social links.
3. **Form delivery:** an endpoint for quote requests.
4. **Company story** (80–120 words), **certifications/standards** (e.g. EN 1176/1177, only if certified), **projects delivered**. These are shown as visible placeholders on /about/.
5. **Real project photography.** No genuine project photos exist, so there is no Projects page. Per the brand book, it must not be filled with renders or stock.
6. **Image rights.** All product images are the manufacturer's renders, captioned "Product visual". Confirm in writing that Dupedia may publish them. Many show rendered children; the brand book forbids children's faces without written consent, so confirm these are acceptable or pick renders without people.
7. **Home page swing photo** (`static/img/home-swings.jpg`, supplied by you on 7 Nov 2026): confirm you hold the rights to publish it, and that it is not presented as a Dupedia project. The brand book also asks for written consent before showing a child's face. The supplied file was 735 px wide; it was enhanced locally to 1470 px with `tools/enhance_photo.py` (2x resample, unsharp mask, contrast). The original is not kept in the site.
7c. **Our factory page** (`/our-factory/`, added 7 Nov 2026 from your brief):
   - **ISO 9001:** shown as a Dupedia-designed badge. ISO does not allow certified companies to use the ISO logo, so add your **certification body's mark**, its name and the certificate number (`iso9001` in `src/config.json`). They show as [TBC] until then.
   - **Safety standard:** the page says "international playground safety requirements". Name the actual standard (for example EN 1176 / EN 1177) in `safetyStandard` once confirmed in writing.
   - **Soliwood is a separate company** (confirmed 7 Nov 2026). The 59-product wooden range is credited to "our manufacturing partner Soliwood, Cairo"; the factory page describes Dupedia's own manufacturing without claiming that range. The factory page stays general about which products the factory makes (owner decision, 7 Nov 2026).
   - **Worker portrait** (`static/img/factory-worker.webp`): cut out and made black and white locally (`tools/cutout.swift`). The Dupedia patch was added digitally (`tools/worker_patch.py`) as a white woven patch per brand book p.15. Confirm the worker's consent to appear on the website.
7d. **Dupedia X FORCE section** (home page, added 8 Nov 2026): every feature listed under "In X FORCE today" is taken from the X FORCE 1.1 docs (order stages, engineering queue and handover packs, production stages with quality inspection and rework, deadline status, teams, chat and notifications, payment verification, sales view of production progress, AI-assisted Market Intelligence). No client portal, live dashboard, logistics module or other AI is claimed. Update `XF_NODES` in `src/build.py` if X FORCE gains features. The engineer photo (`static/img/xforce-field.webp`) was supplied by you; if it is a stock image, keep it unlabelled and confirm the licence.
7e. **Division and showcase photos** (supplied 8 Nov 2026, `static/img/div-*.webp`, `static/img/showcase-fun*.webp`): shown as photos of each kind of space, not as Dupedia projects. Confirm the licence for each, and consent where children are visible. The outdoor-playground photo (`div-playgrounds.webp`) was used at your request on 8 Nov 2026; it appears to show a small maker's badge on the play set, so confirm the licence and that it does not present another brand's product as Dupedia's.
7b. **Data to confirm with the manufacturer:** SW-A402 (published safety zone smaller than its footprint, so it is left out of the planning-guide table), SW-A405 (dimensions repeat SW-Y307's zone), SW-A411 (no genuine photo), and the other items in `src/data/product_flags.json`.
8. **Arabic.** No approved Arabic website copy exists. The build supports `lang`/`dir` per page and the brand's Readex Pro. Add `/ar/` pages and `hreflang` once a native copywriter supplies text. Do not machine-translate.
9. **Service area beyond Egypt.** MENA countries are not mentioned anywhere until confirmed.
10. **Legal review** of the privacy page.

## Arabic website

The build now generates `/ar/` equivalents for every public page, including all product pages. The header globe switches between matching pages and preserves active URL filters and anchors. Arabic uses right-to-left layout and Readex Pro. Copy lives in `src/i18n/ar.json`, technical component terminology in `src/i18n/parts-ar.json`, and generation in `src/i18n/build_ar.py`. Interactive strings are localized by `src/assets/ar-runtime.js`; geometry data and product codes stay unchanged. The downloadable manufacturer catalogue remains its original English PDF. Review builds remain noindex in both languages.
