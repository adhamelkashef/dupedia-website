Playground planning guide How much space does a playground need?

More than the equipment itself. Here is how safety zones work, what the figures in our range say, and what to prepare before you send us a plan.

Plan the zone, not just the equipment.

Every structure in our outdoor range has a published safety zone: the free area the layout must keep around it. It is usually much larger than the structure.

My First Playground (SW-Y301) stands on 4.03 × 1.38 m, about 5.6 m². Its zone is 8.03 × 5.38 m, about 43 m², which is 7.8 times its footprint. If a yard is planned from the footprint, it will not fit.

Before you send a plan

Six things that make a quote faster.

You don’t need all of them to start. A rough sketch with measurements is enough for a first conversation.

  1. LocationCity or area, and whether the site is indoors or outdoors.
  2. Usable area or a site planPDF, DXF, or a photo with rough dimensions. Note trees, drains, levels and access.
  3. Who will use itAge groups, and roughly how many children at once.
  4. Type of spaceOutdoor playground, indoor or soft play, water, or something else.
  5. Target opening dateOr the tender and planning stage you are at.
  6. Budget rangeIf you know it. It helps us suggest the right scale.

Questions we hear often

What is a safety zone?

The free area a layout must keep around a piece of play equipment, as published by its manufacturer. Nothing else goes inside it: no other equipment, no benches, no trees, no paths crossing it.

Does the fit check certify my layout?

No. It checks geometry against the published figures: whether equipment and zones fit your site and stay clear of each other. It is not a safety certification. We review every layout before quoting.

Can a product be turned to fit?

Yes. The fit check on this site tries each product in both orientations, and we do the same when we draw your layout.

Some products say “zone set in your layout”. What does that mean?

The manufacturer publishes a fixed zone for 39 of the 59 products. For the others, the zone is published as an area only (3) or set per site plan (17), so we draw it when we lay out your site.

Which files can I send?

A PDF or DXF site plan is ideal. A photo with rough dimensions is enough to start. If you work in CAD, a DXF export is easiest for us to measure from.

Do you give prices online?

No. Prices depend on the products, the site and the surfacing, so we prepare a quotation for each project.
