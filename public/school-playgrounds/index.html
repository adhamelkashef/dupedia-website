Questions we are asked

Which playground equipment suits each school age?

Every product in our range lists its age group. Early-years units suit ages 2 to 6; climbing and agility sets suit older pupils.

How much space does a school playground need?

More than the equipment itself, because each unit needs a clear safety zone. Our planning guide explains how to measure it.

Can installation happen during school holidays?

Yes. Tell us your term dates and we plan supply and installation around them.
