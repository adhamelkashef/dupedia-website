Questions we are asked

What should a resort kids area include?

Usually an outdoor playground, a shaded or indoor kids club and, where there is a pool, water play. We plan the mix around your guests and space.

Can you work during the season?

Installation is planned around occupancy and guest areas. Tell us your dates.

Which hotels have you worked with?

Our clients include Mövenpick Resorts, Rixos Hotels and Renaissance Cairo Mirage City. See our projects page.
