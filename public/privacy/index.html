What the quote form collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send a quote request we receive the details you type into the form: your name, company, email, phone, role, project type and location, available space, timing, your message and the products on your shortlist.

Why

Only to reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation. We do not sell your details or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Stored on your device

Your shortlist is kept in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent anywhere until you send a request.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.
