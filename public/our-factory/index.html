Illustrative making sequence

01 / 06

Materials in

Timber, steel, fittings and finishes are selected for outdoor play and inspected on arrival. Nothing enters production without passing that check.

02 / 06

Cutting and machining

Parts are cut and machined to the approved drawings for each product code, so every set is made to the same specification.

03 / 06

Sanding and finishing

Edges and surfaces are smoothed for small hands, then finished for sun and weather.

04 / 06

Assembly and fit check

Structures are assembled and checked for fit in the factory, before anything leaves for site.

05 / 06

Final inspection

Each order is inspected against its product specification and signed off under our ISO 9001 procedures.

06 / 06

Pack, deliver, install

Components are packed with their drawings, delivered and installed by our team, then handed over.
