Resort kids area guide Designing a resort kids area.

Families choose resorts partly for the children. A well-planned kids area keeps them busy, safe and happy, and gives parents time to enjoy their stay.

1. Think in zones, not single pieces

The best resort kids areas combine an outdoor playground, a shaded or indoor kids club for the hottest hours and, where there is a pool, water play. Together they cover the whole day.

2. Place it where parents can see it

A playground next to the pool bar, the beach restaurant or a family seating area gets used far more than one hidden behind the gardens. Keep clear sight lines and add comfortable seating and shade for parents.

3. Build for sun, sand and salt

Coastal resorts are hard on equipment. Choose materials and finishes made for strong sun and, near the sea, salt air, and plan a simple inspection and maintenance routine with your engineering team.

4. Match the landscape and furniture

A kids area should look like part of the resort, not an afterthought. Colours, materials and the benches and loungers around it can follow the same design language as the rest of the property.

Key takeaways

  • Combine outdoor play, a kids club and water play to cover the day.
  • Put the kids area where parents can see it.
  • Choose materials for sun and salt air.
  • Match colours and furniture to the property.
