Soft play design guide Designing a soft play area.

A soft play area succeeds when children can play freely, parents can watch comfortably and staff can keep it clean. Plan these from the first sketch.

1. Measure the clear height

Soft play is built upwards, so the clear height under beams, ducts and lights decides how many levels you can have. Measure it before anything else.

2. Separate age zones

Toddlers need a low, separated area with soft shapes and gentle slopes. Older children want height, slides and challenge. Keeping them apart makes the space safer and calmer for both.

3. Plan capacity and flow

Estimate how many children will be inside at peak times and design enough play activities and exits for them. Place the entrance, shoe area and exit so families move through without crowding.

4. Choose materials for safety and cleaning

Use soft parts, nets and flooring that are suitable for indoor play centres, including fire performance requirements, and that can be wiped and cleaned daily. Ask for the documentation for each material.

5. Design for parents

Parents stay longer when they can sit comfortably with a clear view of the play frame. Seating, a café counter and good sight lines turn a play area into a destination.

Key takeaways

  • Clear height decides the number of levels.
  • Separate toddlers from older children.
  • Choose materials suitable for indoor use and easy to clean.
  • Give parents seating with a clear view.
