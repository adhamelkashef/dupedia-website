Questions we are asked

How much does a soft play area cost in Egypt?

It depends on floor area, ceiling height, the number of levels and the theming. Send us your floor plan and we prepare a quotation for your space.

What ceiling height does soft play need?

Multi-level frames need more height than toddler zones. Tell us your clear height and we design the number of levels around it.

Do you install outside Cairo?

Yes. We supply and install across Egypt, including resort destinations.
